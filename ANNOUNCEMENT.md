# DSRS Data Curator Challenge

**Data Science Intern screening challenge**

Start with nothing but a list of investment managers. Fetch their SEC 13F filings,
understand how the data is put together, structure it into a dataset a researcher can
trust, and make it accessible through an agent.

This is the work the role actually involves — find data, understand it, structure it,
make it usable, and explain it. Everything you build is yours to keep and show.

**Repo:** [GiesDSRS/dsrs-curator](https://github.com/GiesDSRS/dsrs-curator)

**Deadline:** Monday, August 24, 2026, 11:59 AM US Central

**Questions:** send them to dsrs@business.illinois.edu. We usually reply within a few
hours, and within a day at the outside. Do not wait on us — make your best judgment
call, record it in `submission/ASSUMPTIONS.md`, and keep working. We would rather see a
documented assumption than a stalled submission.

---

## What you build

Five chapters, all open from day one. The pace is yours.

| | Chapter | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Source** | Fetch the filings from EDGAR — at scale, without getting blocked |
| 2 | **Interrogate** | Understand how the data is structured and what it can support |
| 3 | **Structure** | Parse it into the dataset specification |
| 4 | **Serve** | Make the data accessible through an agent |
| 5 | **Show** | A 3-minute demo |

Two optional bonuses, 5 points each, once the core is complete.

The full specification, per-chapter rubrics, and the output schema are in the repo.

## Getting your repo

1. **Sign in to GitHub**, then
   [create your repository from this template](https://github.com/GiesDSRS/dsrs-curator/generate)
2. Name it, set visibility to **Private**, and create it
3. In your new repo: **Settings → Collaborators → Add people → `dsrsBOT`**, Read access
4. Clone it and start

Use the link above rather than the **Fork** button. A fork of a public repository is
always public, and your work needs to stay private until results are announced.

Step 3 is the one people forget. Without it we cannot read your repository, and there
is no submission to grade.

---

## Submitting

You submit one link: your GitHub repository URL.

Everything else lives in the repo. Your video link, your name, and your NetID go in
`submission/SUBMISSION.md`, which is part of what you commit.

Your **code** is not frozen at submission. We take the last commit made before the
deadline, so keep committing and pushing right up to it — and please commit as you work
rather than in one push at the end.

---

## The video

Under 3 minutes, showing your pipeline running from a cold start and your agent
answering a question. Show a live run, not just a finished interface. You do not need
to appear on camera — a screen recording with narration is all we are after.

Upload to **Illinois MediaSpace**: https://mediaspace.illinois.edu/upload/media

Set visibility to **Unlisted**, then paste the link into `submission/SUBMISSION.md`.

**A submission without a video is incomplete and is not graded.**

## On AI assistance

We encourage its usage — you are applying to build agentic tooling.

Declare what you used in `submission/AI_USAGE.md`. We are not scoring the amount. We
are checking that you can account for your own work, which is also what the walkthrough
is for.

## What happens next

1. **Automated grading** — every submission is evaluated identically. Each chapter has
   its own rubric in `/docs`; review them before you start
2. **Review** — code and video, for submissions that clear the automated gates
3. **Interviews** — a technical and behavioral interview with the DSRS team, including
   a walkthrough of your own code
