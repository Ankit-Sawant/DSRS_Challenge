# 5 · Show

**5 points** · Produces a video link in `submission/SUBMISSION.md`

---

> "Last thing, and it's the one people underrate.
>
> Record yourself running it. Three minutes, start to finish — kick off the pipeline
> cold, let it go, then ask the agent something and show what comes back. Talk over it
> as you go: what it's doing, why you built it that way, anything you'd change.
>
> Half of this job is explaining your work to someone who wasn't there when you did it.
> The researcher will watch this, and so will we. If your pipeline does something clever,
> this is where we find out — a good run looks the same as a lucky one from the outside.
>
> Don't over-produce it. One take is fine. We'd rather see the real thing than a polished
> reconstruction."

---

## What to record

**Under 3 minutes.** We stop watching at three, so put the important parts first.

Show two things running:

**Your pipeline, from a cold start.** Not the finished output — the actual run. Clear
your outputs first, start it, and let it go. If it takes longer than the video allows,
say so and cut ahead; we care that it runs, not that we watch every second of it.

**Your agent answering a question.** Ask it something real, show the answer and its
sources. If you have time for a second question that it *cannot* answer, show that too —
how a system fails is more informative than how it succeeds.

Narrate as you go. Not a script — just say what is happening and why you built it that
way. Where you made a call that could have gone differently, say so.

You do not need to appear on camera. A screen recording with narration is all we are
after.

---

## Uploading

Upload to **Illinois MediaSpace**: https://mediaspace.illinois.edu/upload/media

Set visibility to **Unlisted** — hidden from public galleries, viewable by anyone with
the link. Then paste that link into `submission/SUBMISSION.md`.

Check the link in a private browser window before you submit it. A video nobody can open
is a video nobody can grade.

**A submission without a video is incomplete and is not graded.** This is the one
requirement with no partial credit.

---

## How this chapter is graded

| | Points |
|---|---|
| **It runs** — a real cold-start execution, not a walkthrough of finished output | 2 |
| **The agent works** — a question asked and answered, sources shown | 1 |
| **You explain it** — what it does, why you built it that way, what you would change | 2 |

Production quality is not graded. Clarity is.

## One more thing

We may share your video publicly to show what candidates built. If you would rather we
did not, say so in `submission/SUBMISSION.md` — it costs you nothing.
